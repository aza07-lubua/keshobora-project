How Our Program Works

Three simple steps take a shy student to a confident young leader.

1

Join a Track

Choose the program that fits your age and level, then join a cohort at your school or a nearby community centre.

2

Complete Challenges

Work through hands-on missions—speeches, team projects, and community activities—guided by trained facilitators.

3

Earn Recognition

Collect badges and certificates as you grow, graduating with a portfolio that showcases your leadership journey.
